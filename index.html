xAI's always-on AI teammates — each Bot gets a persistent cloud computer with browser, filesystem, and terminal to work across apps like a human would.

Shaped teammate-style UX: parallel Bots that coordinate in group chats, demonstration-learned routines, and handoffs so you're not the router between tools.
